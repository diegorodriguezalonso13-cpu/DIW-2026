STEEL BALL RUN RACE

The biggest horse race in the history, racing across the United States of America, from San Diego to New York City.

Our objectives

Test human limits

There is no evidence that any human has ever traveled such a distance with the same horse in such a short time, we'd like to reward the participants that are able to do so.

Reward skills of the participants

Besides the winning prize, after completing a stage between the top ten, the participants will get a moneary prize and a time bonus for the first one

Complete protection

Every and all participant will get a complete protection for the entire race, even bringing them back home safely if they retire.

About us

Steel Co. was created by Steven Steel in 1890, with the purpose of creating a horse race that would cross the entire United States of America.

“This is a race to reward the pioneer spirit of humanity .”
